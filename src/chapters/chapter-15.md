# 15. The Killer

When Max woke up, it was raining.

_Drip. Drip. Drip._

Then, it clicked.

Max ran down to get her phone. She dialed Riana's landline number, "_**469**_".

"Riana. Emergency meeting. Come over quickly. Call Jack too. Bye!"

Max just threw the words that came to her mind. She didn't even give Riana a chance to speak.

After a while, maybe 20 minutes, Jack and Riana arrived with wet umbrellas. They all sat at the dining table.

Max spoke first, "I think I know who the killer is and where he is."

Riana and Jack gasped.

"Remember the first clue we got? _**Mardley**_. Second clue, _**Bury**_. That put together is _**Mardleybury**_, my hometown".

"Remember our list? Evan Ikob, Judy Ikob, Jason Ikob, and Oliver Avinap".

"_**Evan... is the killer**_".

Riana and Jack looked at Max with confused faces.

"You don't believe me about the Evan part. Do you?... Okay, let me explain. See, my name is _Maxima Evans_. My last name is after my uncle, and he gave us the clue Mardleybury. So I have reason to suspect that my uncle Evan is the killer and that he is holding my father at my old house."

Riana and Jack were breathless.

Jack realized and said, "Elizabeth is your cousin!. Evan Ikob's daughter is Elizabeth. She's your cousin."

Max didn't know how to react. But she did say, "I will kill that old jerk, if it's the last thing I do."

Riana added to the conversation.

"Do you still have those... guns?"

Max smiled a naughty, wide grin and said, "I have something better."

Everyone stood up.

Max twisted the fruit bowl in the center. Off to the right, something started to tweak and turn, revealing a secret door inside. Max switched on the light and invited them.

When they saw the room, they were flabbergasted.

There were Snipers, Shotguns, Pistols, Rifles, Uzis. Every single weapon you could dream of. Max came out with 3 belts, each holding all types of guns, a pocket knife, and a walkie-talkie.

Max taught them how to use each gun, and they were all set.

Jack and Riana asked, "Isn't this crazy?"

Max replied, "It is really crazy. But we are going to bring the killer to justice".

"_**We are going to murder a murderer !**_"
