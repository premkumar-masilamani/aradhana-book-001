# 6. The Phone Call

"Oliver was Elizabeth's son. Elizabeth married Victor Avinap. Oliver wasn't always... _social_. He hated everybody, and vice versa. He mostly hated his parents and wanted money. Victor was a CEO of a polyester company. He was very well paid. They lived comfortably until Elizabeth's death.", Max took a breath and continued…

"Oliver knew Emily. When he was in 9th grade, Emily was in 1st grade. He used to pick on her, fool her, mock her.", Max said disappointingly.

"He lied... Luke lied!... Louis Amiaga isn't real!. He must have been very loyal to whoever he's working for!" Jack realized.

"We're so stupid!… Why, why, why, why, why did we let him free?!" Riana raged.

"Guys, my phone is ringing... The number is anonymous," Max picked up the phone and put it on speaker.

"_Maxima Evans, what do you think you're doing?. Stop investigating, or else something bad will happen, as two murders has happened already. Don't think, I won't do anything to you guys_", the phone said in a deep male voice.

Before Max could say anything, the phone ended in three loud beeps.

The phone rang again.

"I forgot to mention I know your friends inside out - Riana Collin and Jackson Villivan."

"Nooo, nooo, nooo!" Riana panicked.

"What should we do?" Jack asked in fear.

"I... Don't... know... "
