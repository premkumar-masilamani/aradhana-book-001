# Chapter 13: All we can do is pray

The first blink of light came out.

"Ughh!" Max fell on the floor.

Today was the day they interviewed Evan.

She pulled herself away from the floor and went to the bathroom. Something isn't right... Max noticed dark, dried-up blood just below her hair.

"What the- ... huh?". She quickly brushed her teeth and went downstairs. It was so messy. The gun on the floor was still there.

She went to the sofa, where she fainted. There she saw it.

The culprit. A small glass cup.

She couldn't tell if it was hers or not.

"_What shall I do to you?",_ she thought in her head.

The next thing you know, she started gluing it. She noticed the photo on the cup. It was Max and her father in...

"Mardleybury!" Max said, relieved.

On the other side, it said...

> *Hello again, idiot.*<br/>
> *Here's your next clue.*<br/>
> *Call both your friends.*<br/>
> *Give them the cue.*

Max came on a video call with Riana and Jack.

Max spoke first.

"Guys! Big news! The place in the first clue was Mardleybury! I grew up there, and the murderer gave me this poem. It said, _Hello again, idiot. Here's your next clue. Call both your friends. Give them the cue_."

Next, Riana spoke. "I got a poem too"

> *Spices and meat.*<br/>
> *Sweet, sweet smells.*<br/>
> *Call your friend Max.*<br/>
> *Get her some help.*

Lastly Jack. "I got one too!"

> *You're a scared boy.*<br/>
> *But you reach the high shelves.*<br/>
> *A girl will find someone.*<br/>
> *Who needs your help.*

Before Max cut the call, she said, "Both of you guys bailed on school, right? Come to my house. No one is home."

"What about your mom? She talks to all our moms", Jack asked, confused.

"Oh! She went out of town... to find a job"

"Guys! Guys! Guys!… Someone is chasing me… Help Ple—", Riana's video switched off.

"Max, what do we do!"

"We can't do anything!.. W-We can't do anything!"

"We have to sit and wait?. Are you crazy?"

"I mean, yeah. We don't know which road she's coming from. We don't know if she's pranking us. We don't know if she's close or far."

"Can't we track her phone or something?"

"No, I don't have that kind of electronics."

"Oh... God..."

"All we can do is pray."
