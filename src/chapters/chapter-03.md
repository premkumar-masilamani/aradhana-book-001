# Chapter 3: The Medicine

"We have to make a plan without Jack" Max said anxiously.

"No, I can come" Jack said.

"The bullet barely grazed his lower ear. It can heal in a week or three days if he puts medicine.", said Riana.

"Ok, put the medicine," Jack said in fear.

"Jack, it will burn a little", Riana said.

"Can you hold my hand, or better a soft toy?"

"Sure, here you go", Max handed over a soft toy.

Max could see fear in Jack's eyes as Riana took the first-aid kit and a white tube and swab, and as she slowly and carefully squeezed it onto the swab, a clear paste came out. Max could see Jack gripping the toy a little harder. Riana brought the swab closer, and closer, and closer.

"Jack, what this will do is it will burn a little skin and harden, like a scab, but faster. Do you want a countdown?" Riana asked.

"Yes," Jack said nervously.

"3", he gripped tighter

"2", and tighter

"1", and tighter.

His veins started popping as she applied it.

He cried.

It was finally done.

He started cooling down.

He took a breath.

It was over.

They rested for another hour.

Riana's mother called, so did Jack's.

They both said they would stay for the next two nights.

"Ok, we have 3 days and 2 nights to solve this," Max complained.

"Wait, Max, if the killer tried to take out Jack, that means he must know you. Because the killer knew that Jack was important... to you", says Riana.

"Riana, what you are saying is that I know the killer and that he murdered a young woman and almost killed Jack?" Max was confused.

"Yeah, that is correct."

They finished off dinner and went to sleep.

Just as Max was about to drift off...

A thud and glass breaking.

Someone is in the house.
