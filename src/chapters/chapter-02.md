# Chapter 2: The Bloody Ear

"Max, please just leave this to the police," Jack said.

Riana replied, "I agree with Max. The police won't do anything."

"We have to construct a plan," Max said.

"We have less than 4 hours to see the body and how the murderer killed her," Riana added.

Max asked, "Jack, are you in?"

Jack hesitated, but agreed.

"Ok, here's what's going to happen. Jack, you're going to distract the guards by screaming '_I found a clue_!' and Riana, you're going to distract the other guards by screaming '_Help, help! There's a snake!_' Within that time, I can go take at least five polaroids, got it?" Max said.

Riana exclaimed, "Yes!" and Jack shrugged and said, "Yes."

They walked to the national park, and Jack spit up and screamed what he had to say, and Riana said so too. All the guards were gone; this was Max's chance to get the polaroids.

The silence brewed up anxiety inside her.

She heard a gunshot. A gunshot.

She saw Jack... running up to her, cupping his ears. "I just got shot in the ear! Call Riana; we have to go! The killer is here!" Max cried for Riana, and they ran home. Fortunately, neither Riana nor Max was hurt.

They reached home. Max's hand was covered in Jack's blood.

"Oh my god, that hurts so bad!" Jack cried.

Riana responded, "Calm down, take a deep breath."

"Why-ahhh! What did you put in my ear?!"

"A disinfectant."

Max was pacing around the house, taking in what just happened.

"Guys! Guys! I know it now. The killer doesn't want us to investigate; he gave us a warning not to," Max figured.

"Okay, so what now?"
