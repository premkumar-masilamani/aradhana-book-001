# Chapter 8: The Note

While they were driving to **Saint Nicolas Church**,

Jack asked, "Max, are you completely sure that this is a good—"

Max replied by flooring the gas pedal. "There is your answer".

Jack wore a terrified look in his face.

"Riana, got it?", Max asked

"Yup. Right in my hands."

Jack looked at the backseat to see Riana holding a roll of duct tape.

They soon arrived at the Saint Nicolas Church. They taped all the cameras with precision.

"Guys, find and pull and tug and turn and push everything," Max ordered.

They searched for a while until Riana moved a photo with **Jesus Christ** on it. The tile started to shake and move, revealing a dark staircase leading into a black hole. She moved it to another side, and lights popped on.

"Guys, I'm a genius!", says Riana excitedly.

They lowered themselves into the staircase. On the table in the centre, a folded note was there. It read:

<hr>

_Hello again, Idiots._

_If you're wondering what my name is, the first five letters of the last name of the man in the photo you moved. Well dummies, your first clue is_ _**Mardley**_

_Bad luck,_

_You know the name_

<hr>

They thought for a while. Riana was the first one to speak.

"Chris..."

"What?", Jack asked.

"Chris is the master's name.", Max realized.

They reached home.

Max thought and murmured _**Mardley**_ over and over again.

"Guys, this folded note isn't paper. It's something else."

Jack ran the note in water.

"Jack! What are you doing!", Riana runs towards him.

The writing disappeared, and a photo appeared.
