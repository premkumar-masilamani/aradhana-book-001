# 14. The Stitches

After a few minutes, there was a thud on the door.

A silhouette of Riana.

Then it swung so hard that Max almost fell down.

Standing in the door frame was Riana, gripping her arm a few inches above her elbow.

Bloody. So, so bloody.

"Riana!" Max said in a relieved but worried, wailing voice.

"W-What happened, h-how can someone do this to you?"

"I-I need stitches, but first I clean the wound", Riana was in pain, but she spoke quite elegantly.

"Riana, that's not a wound. It's a gash", Jack tried to cheer her up.

"Riana, guide me through it", Max took responsibility because she knew Jack wouldn't.

Jack sat in silence while Max and Riana talked.

"First, wash the gash with warm water, Max", Max did.

"Now open the first-aid kit and get me the small bottle inside with blue liquid and a cotton ball", Max obeyed.

"Tip the alcohol onto the cotton ball for 3 seconds", Max listened.

Jack sat there amazed at how these two girls could work.

"Now rub it softly on the gash."

Riana whimpered.

"Now take the 5 ml needle and take 3 ml from the small bottle, the size of your pinky and inject above and below the wou-, sorry, gash."

"Riana, h-how do I do this?. I-I've never done it before." Max's anxiety started to blow up like a balloon.

"Max, just put the needle in. Don't worry, I'll be fine."

Max shut up and obeyed.

"Let the anesthesia settle down", Riana let out a breath.

It was soon time for stitching. It was around 4:00 PM.

Max prepared while waiting for the anesthesia to kick in. She found the needle and thread... She threaded it... And she placed it down.

Riana taught Max the pattern, and preparation was... done.

Max put on plastic gloves, and it was time to stitch.

Even though Jack wasn't the patient getting stitches, he still groaned.

Riana didn't even pay attention to the stitching because she had dozed off; she didn't feel an ounce of pain, it seemed.

Max sooner or later finished stitching and woke Riana up.

5:00 PM., huh.

It was by now that they would come back from school.

Max sent Jack and Riana home, washed her hands, cleaned up the blood and the kitchen, put everything back in its place, and dozed off for a long, long time.
