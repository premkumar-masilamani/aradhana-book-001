# Chapter 9: The Poem

Max was amazed. She couldn't believe that somehow Jack had found out this clue.

It was a photo of child dressed as King Henry VIII, a very old one.

"It was our fancy dress competition. I was Jane Seymour and she was Catherine Parr. The 3rd and 6th wives of King Henry VIII, along with a few other classmates", Max explained.

Riana added, "The first one, Catherine of Aragon was divorced. The second wife, Anne Boleyn was beheaded. The third one, Jane Seymour died of childbirth. The fourth one, Anne of Cleves was divorced. The fifth one, Catherine Howard was beheaded. The last one, Catherine Parr survived until King Henry VIII died"

Jack stood silently until...

"Divorced, Beheaded, Died, Divorced, Beheaded, Survived. It rhymes. Does your father do poetry?", said to Max.

"Yes, he does. Why?"

"Can you show me one of his poems, please?"

Max showed one of his recent poems:

> *First the mouth, no drop of sound.*\
> *Next the eyes, not a gleam of light.*\
> *Next the neck with a long gray line.*\
> *They fall into your hands, they seem fine.*

Jack read it out loud.

"Do-Do you know what th-th-th-this means, Max?" Riana asked, trembling...

I know exactly what this means.
