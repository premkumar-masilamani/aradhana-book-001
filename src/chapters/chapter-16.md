# 16. Boom

Jack drove. Max talked. Riana was calm.

"My house in Mardleybury has two entrances. I'll take the back entrance. You two take the front one", said Max.

Riana asked, "Why two and one?"

Max answered, "_I have way more experience_".

Jack nodded in approval.

"Jack, park here", Max said.

The house was about 40 feet from the car. Max was near the back entrance. She walked inside. No signs of anybody, until someone pulled her hair.

Before she could scream, he put his hand over her mouth. The person whispered, "Call your friends into the living room."

Max obeyed, "Guys! Come into the living room."

When her friends walked into the living room, the killer pushed Max alongside Riana and Jack.

"Well, well, well… what a surprise!", the murderer said sarcastically.

"Where is my father, Evan?"

"Oh no!.. You don't respect your beloved uncle, Max?"

Then, from the side, her dad came into view.

"Dad!" Max shrieked.

"Don't come a step closer," the person said.

Max hinted to Riana and Jack to point their pistol at this person.

Max asked, "Why did you kill my beloved cousin? Why did you unnecessarily murder Emily Viana? Why kidnap my father and plant the evidence, Evan?"

This mystery person took off his mask. It was Evan. Max was right.

"Max, Max, Max... You have two guns pointed at me. So I'll tell you everything", said the murderer without a drop of fear.

"See... when I wanted a boy, my wife had a girl. I couldn't keep up with girls and their needs. So after she reached a certain age, I kicked her out. I don't know where she got so much money, but eventually she went to college. But before I kicked her out, I adopted Jason to make her life hell, but when I saw her leading such a pleasant life, it had to change. She was supposed to be miserable. So I asked her _parent-hating_ son to give her an _early-passing-away_ ceremony."

"So then, I was free with my son Jason and my wife. But then you were born; from the look of your face I knew you-,... wait... the timeline is not correct. _**After**_ you were born, Oliver gave Elizabeth an _early-passing-away_ ceremony. I knew you would support Elizabeth; that's why I asked him to. Now I wanted to play. I kidnapped your father so you guys would play along. And tada! Here we are…"

"Go ahead, take your father home. But before that, I ask for one thing."

"A classic fight, no weapons."

Max smiled along with Riana and Jack.

"You know Evan?. There's one thing you didn't know about us. We're strong 11th graders who know how to fight a good fight. We treat each other like siblings, for goodness sake. So if you want to pick a fight with us, it's your choice of bad luck."

These words made Evan anxious.

Max ran and jumped and gave a kick to Evan's stomach before going back a little.

He exchanged a blow to Max's face.

"How dare you!" Riana shouted and started punching his face left to right, before Max pulled her away.

Jack's moment had finally come. Max and Riana held Evan by his arms, and Jack said, "This is what you get for taking the lives of two innocent girls and kidnapping a beloved Father!"

Jack kicked him on the chest so hard that Evan's whole body went flying and hit the wall.

All four of them were outside: Max, Riana, Jack, and Max's father, Owens.

Suddenly, there was an explosion, and no one was fazed except Owens.

"What happened to our house?"

"It's always good to have plan B, Dad… Boom…", Max made an explosive gesture with her hands.

They got in the car, went home, and got a good night's rest.

Tomorrow, they're giving a speech for England.
