# Chapter 7: The Crypt

"What do you mean, you don't know?" Jack questioned.

"I don't know," Max said lifelessly.

"Okay... No biggie, we can handle this", Riana said without meaning the words.

"How Riana!.. How!.. The killer is still out there. Jack got shot in the ear. We almost died, and we made a stupid decision to let Luke go!. So tell me... How do we fix it?" Max said with rage and fear in her eyes.

"Where's your dad?" Jack asked.

"He's here somewhere!... Dad!", No response.

"Dad!...". No response.

"Dad!" Max panicked.

They started searching the house. No dad to be found.

"Guys, I'm getting a call, anonymous." Jack put it on speaker.

"Hello, this one's for all of you. I faked Emily's death, and I took your dad. Come find me... Your first clue is at the crypt", the voice from the phone said.

"How did you do this?", Max asked the phone a question before he hung up.

"Oh... You still don't know? The so-called _Luke guy_ didn't tell you? Okay. It was a two-man process. He sneaked through the main door by picking the lock; he duct-taped your dad's mouth first and lowered him out the window; the other guy got him and signaled to break the glass. Only when you heard the sound of the glass breaking, you got suspicious. You caught him and sent him away like you would, idiots", the voice from the phone giggled.

"We finally have something to do and a lead", Max said, excited.

"Max... Your dad-duh is missing", Jack said, confused.

Max smirked and said, "Get in the car. We're going to the crypt."
