# 4. The Interrogation

"_No no no no no..._ ", Max thought in her head.

Max immediately woke up Riana and Jack. She explained.

"How. Will. We. Fight. The. Killer!?" Riana quietly exclaimed.

"Guys, don't judge me", as she opened her drawer and took a layer of wood, Riana and Jack saw... some knives... and three revolvers.

"Max, why on earth do you have this?" Jack questioned.

"Hehe. Just for emergency... Both of you take one knife and a gun."

Jack obediently took a gun and a knife, and Riana confidently took them too.

Max handed over a thick rope to each person. She got ready herself.

Max led them down the stairs. They could see a long shadow standing in the hallway.

Max screamed, "Drop your weapon! You are one vs three, DROP YOUR WEAPON!"

He dropped it.

"Turn toward us."

He turned.

"Now sit down on that chair."

He sat.

Riana and Jack started tying his chest, elbows, wrists, hips, knees, and ankles.

Max questioned, "Who are you? Why are you here? Why did you shoot Jack? Why did you kill that girl!?"

"I'm not telling you anything."

"Last warning," Max replied calmly.

"I said... I'm not telling you anythi—" Riana shot him in the arm.

Max secretly praised her.

"Aghh... Okay!.. I'll tell you!... I work for Louis Smith. I don't know anything else."

Max was pleased.

"Listen to me. If a word of this goes to the police, we won't shoot your arm... we'll shoot your head." Jack threatened.

They threw him out of the house.

Riana searched for Louis Smith.

"Max, Jack, there's no information on Louis Smith at all on the internet."

Max was surprised.

Jack put on the news...

Something horrible had happened.
