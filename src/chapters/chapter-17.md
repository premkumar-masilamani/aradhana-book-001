# 17. Hello, England

Max was backstage with Riana and Jack. They could hear the crowd murmuring and all the telecasters ready to telecast. The person next to Max said, "You guys are on in five seconds".

Max, Riana, and Jack walked onto the stage. The whole crowd cheered.

"Hello, England! We're brought here today because of two murders. Elizabeth Brook and Emily Viara. Two innocent people. They led good lives but met an early end. But we... we stopped it from happening again! You might wonder who we are, so let's do an introduction. I am Maxima Evans, but I am going to change my name to Maxima Owens, after my father"

"Hello everyone, I am Jackson Villivan"

"Good morning, I am Riana Collin"

"These heroes and heroines saved England. Say, Jack over here, he got shot. Riana got stitches and I got a blow to the face", said Max.

"I think we should all appreciate our loved ones, friends, and family for being there for us. Thank you".

"Wait," a voice said.

When that voice came into view, the trio saw the Chief of Police.

"Before you go, I want to give you guys an offer. Will you guys become head investigators of the police force?"

The trio grinned, "Um, absolutely"

The crowd cheered.

It was like heaven for Max. She and her best friends became head investigators. Her mom came back, and they all lived as a family together.

After getting off stage, Jack asked Riana and Max, "Do you want to get ramen?"

Max answered, "Yes".

Riana answered, "After all that, I think we deserve a good bowl of ramen".

Max said, "What are you waiting for? Let's go!"
