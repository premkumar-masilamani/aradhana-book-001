# 10. The Murderer

"My father is the murderer! But how, why him?", Max said sad and confused.

The door shifted open... It was just the wind, or so she thought. Her Father... stood in front of her.

"Hey guys, I'm home!"

"You're the murderer." Max's words barely came out.

"What?" said her father, confused

"You're the murderer!" Max wiped her eyes.

"What! No! No! No! Honey, you got it all wrong!"

"Then what's this!" Max held up the paper.

Her father stood silently.

"What is it? What is it! Huh?!" Max shouted at her father.

Max whispered to Riana to call the police.

When the police arrived, they were in the same position. Jack filled in details about him. Riana explained what happened.

Max just sat, looking forward. She saw the disappointment on her father's face when he was loaded into the car.

Her head started spinning. The things around her seemed to be moving. The red and blue lights on the police car seemed to merge, but not mix.

She started to feel weak. She fell down and couldn't get up.

It was pitch black now.
