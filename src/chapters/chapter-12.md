# 12. The First Hallucination

Max screamed. Riana jerked up. Jack almost crashed.

"What - what's wrong?" Riana panicked.

"Th - there was a giant truck he- headed to crash into us", Max freaked out.

"There was no truck", Jack interrupted.

"Max, are you _Okay_?", asks Riana.

"Yeah, yeah, I'm fine."

**Hallucinations.**

Weird.

It happened to her mother a week before she died.

Her mother's first hallucination was that she saw the exact same truck driver getting drunk along with a few other drivers, and threw himself into the truck.

The second hallucination came a few hours before she was hit. That driver was drunk and driving in the opposite lane, the wrong one.

She was planning to go to a party but hesitated because, in her hallucination, the driver was heading down the same lane she was headed to.

When Jack was driving Max to her house. Max ran this story in her head over and over and eventually fell asleep.

She heard a faint sound calling "Max", then a little louder, and a little louder, and finally...

"Max!"

She jerked up. Jack and Riana were parked outside her house.

"Thanks guys. Return the car tomorrow. I'll see you at school, bye !", Max said sleepily and waved.

She changed into her pink pajamas and got cozy in her bed.

It was a long weekend.
